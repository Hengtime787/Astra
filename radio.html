About Me

I'm Matthew [KM7IBY], and I'm an amateur radio technician. I received my HAM License in October, 2026. I'm based from Missoula MT, and I listen to local repeaters in the area (most of them are run by HARC), and I often monitor the national calling frequency (146.520).

If you are working on getting a HAM license, or just meeting some awesome people, consider reaching out to the WEARC team in New Jersey. They administered my HAM Exam, and were incredibly kind and awesome.
