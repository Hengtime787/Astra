Bicro Mit: Battle Pro Max Ultra

What is Bicro Mit: Battle Pro Max Ultra?

A Custom Coded Game Inspired by Table Turf Battle

In this refined two player game, you battle to be able to claim your squares. The Microsoft Micro:Bit™ creates a thrilling enviroment to randomly select virtual cards in decks of your choosing! You will never know what's coming next!

  1. This game will draw many players through pure fun and addiction. Players will have a lottery like feeling when they draw a new card from the exciting new Micro: Bit™ V2. The convenient battery packs can power the Micro:Bit™ for a long time, allowing for long gameplay. The players will buy this game for sure, because of pure fun.

Tech Tip:

Make sure you are fast with your drawing. You can steal the other person's square.
