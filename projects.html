Antimatter

-- Finished + More Planned --
The Antimatter Initiative is an open-source command line tool that puts popular linux tools, such as playerctl into one convenient place. Tools such as theming are coming soon.

Atlas

-- WIP | ETA November --
The Atlas Initiative is aimed to be an ultra fast, yet smart digital assistant. Coded with python, it uses offline local data only, until it absolutely needs to. Integrations with Google Maps, Youtube Music, and other services are also planned.
