Atlas

-- WIP | ETA November --
The Atlas Initiative is aimed to be an ultra fast, yet smart digital assistant. Coded with Kotlin, it uses offline local data only, until it absolutely needs to touch the internet. Integrations with Google Maps, Youtube Music, and other services are also planned. It's personality engine is still TBD, but will provide a rich and AI slop free experience, with modern features, such as smart response types and slim internet searches and queries.
