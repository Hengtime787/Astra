What is Atlas?

Atlas is a voice assistant designed to be fast, local, secure, and smart, with tons of integrations, a lively personality that only talks when necessary, and customisable OSDs. Atlas will be compatible with any platform supporting KMP, and will have the performance capabilities to run on a first generation Raspberry Pi Zero.

Fun Fact: Atlas'' name was inspired by the Atlas from No Man's Sky

NOTE: Atlas is still a work in progress, and is expected to be completed in November. Updates are shown at Atlas on Github, and full devlogs (until September 30th) can be found at Atlas on Stardance.
