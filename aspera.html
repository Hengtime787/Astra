What is Aspera?

Aspera (formerly known as Atlas) is aimed to be the fastest and most efficient voice assistant. Most voice assistants, like Siri and Google

In Android Studio benchmarks, Aspera completed and executed it's actions under human reaction time, creating a virtually instant experience.

Origins

Aspera started as a humble side project, out of my frustration with Google Assistant. I wanted to find some voice assistant/agent that was:

  • Free
  • Fast/Efficient
  • Enviromentally Friendly
  • Run by an ethical company (or solo project)
  • Useful (wide feature variety)

Innovation

Aspera has a completely unique, efficient, and innovative engine behind all of it's actions. Aspera uses no AI or Language Models, which substantially improves performence, as well as compatibility; when practically every other voice assistant just pipes their users requests to an LLM, which also poses a potential security risk. This poses a unique challenge for Aspera to solve: It needs to figure out the user's intent, and any other relevant information to pass along, as well as actually executing the action, and generating a response; all while staying on-device, without proprietary services, and without AI. Aspera keeps all of your data, preferences, and requests offline. This enhances the speed and security of Aspera. Usually when making any local-first application, developers compromise on app size and usage on storage (which often impacts speed and resource usage as well), but Aspera is designed around keeping storage usage low, and speed quick, posing another development challenge. Using a quantisized intent engine, along with custom in-house systems and code, Aspera is able to run quickly and efficiently, offline, without substantially impacting storage space.
